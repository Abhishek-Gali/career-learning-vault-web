How Your 24/7 Keep-Alive Architecture Works

1. Render Free Tier spins down instances after 15 minutes of zero traffic.

2. Your Cron Job (on cron-job.org) sends an HTTP GET request to https://<your-app>.onrender.com/api/health every 10 minutes.

3. Because Render receives traffic every 10 minutes (less than the 15-minute timeout), the instance NEVER goes to sleep!

4. Result: Zero cold starts, immediate <100ms response time, 100% free with zero credit card required!
